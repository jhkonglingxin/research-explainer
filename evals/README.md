# Synthetic evaluation scenarios

`cases.json` contains invented source material and user requests. None of the cases is scientific evidence or a real participant record.

To evaluate a revision, give an independent agent the skill and a case, without telling it the expected answer. Save the actual explanation, then review it against the source and the requested language, audience, and length. An external model runner or paid service is not required by this repository.

Review these material behaviors:

- Exact quantities and units remain correct; odds, probability, relative change, and percentage points are not conflated.
- Uncertainty and adjusted versus nominal significance are preserved.
- Independent experimental units and clustered or repeated observations are distinguished.
- Qualitative themes and selected materials do not become population prevalence or causal proof.
- Mathematical assumptions remain visible; a theorem is not presented as physical validation.
- Benchmark and simulation results retain their tested conditions and validation gaps.
- Conflicting or opposite-direction results are not omitted for a simpler story.
- Missing images, source sections, or error-bar definitions are not invented.
- The explanation fits the user's requested language and length without unnecessary headings or patronizing wording.

No checklist can mechanically establish the correctness of every scientific interpretation. Record failures, correct only demonstrated problems, and rerun the affected scenarios when a revision changes behavior.
